# UAE Credit Card Benefits Summary

## 1. ADCB Credit Cards

### ADCB 365 Cashback Credit Card
- **Cashback:** Earn up to **6% cashback** on everyday spends.
- **Monthly Limit:** Up to **AED 1,000** in cashback per month.
- **Welcome Bonus:** **AED 365**.
- **Additional Benefits:**
  - Hotel discounts.
  - Purchase protection and Extended Warranty.
  - Card loan and 0% interest payment plans.

### Lulu Credit Cards
**Lulu Platinum Credit Card**
- **Rewards:** Earn up to **8 LuLu Points** for every AED spent.
- **Annual Fee:** **Free for life**.
- **Travel:** Complimentary airport lounge access.
- **Entertainment:** Buy 1 Get 1 Free movie tickets.
- **Additional Benefits:** Dining/car ride discounts, 0% interest plans.

**Lulu Titanium Credit Card**
- **Rewards:** Earn up to **3.5 LuLu Points** for every AED spent.
- **Annual Fee:** **Free for life**.
- **Additional Benefits:** Dining/car ride discounts, 0% interest plans.

### Talabat ADCB Credit Card
- **Talabat Perks:**
  - **35% back** on Talabat orders.
  - **Unlimited free delivery** on Talabat orders.
- **Welcome Bonus:** Up to **AED 750**.
- **Lifestyle & Travel:**
  - Access to airport lounges.
  - Personalized card design.
  - Hotel discounts.
- **Financial:** 0% installment plans available.

## 2. Emirates Islamic Credit Cards

### Emirates Islamic Amazon World Credit Card
- **Annual Fee:** **Free for Life**.
- **Welcome Bonus:** Up to **AED 1,000** (spend AED 10k in first 60 days).
- **Rewards:** **Up to 6% back** on Amazon.ae purchases (Unlimited).
- **Lifestyle & Travel:**
  - **Lounges:** 8 complimentary visits worldwide.
  - **Movies:** Up to 2 "Buy 1 Get 1" tickets/month.
  - **Golf:** 2 rounds/month.
  - **Dining:** 2 free items at Costa Coffee (Dubai airports).
  - **Transport:** 1 free airport ride with Careem/year.
  - **Valet:** 1 free service/month.

### Emirates Islamic Switch Cashback Credit Card
- **Annual Fee:** Free 1st year; 2nd year waived on AED 30k spend (else AED 299).
- **Welcome Bonus:** **AED 500** (spend AED 15k in first 60 days).
- **Cashback Categories (Switch Monthly):**
  - **Lifestyle Option:** 8% Fuel, 4% Supermarkets/Dining/Education.
  - **Travel Option:** 4% Airlines/Hotels/Dining.
  - **Other:** Up to 1% on other spends.
- **Lifestyle:**
  - **Golf:** 2 rounds/month.
  - **Movies:** 2 "Buy 1 Get 1" tickets/month.
  - **Valet:** 1 free service/month.

### Emirates Islamic Cashback Plus Credit Card
- **Annual Fee:** Free 1st year (spend AED 5k in 90 days); 2nd year waived on AED 30k spend (else AED 299).
- **Cashback:** **Up to 10%** on Groceries, Education, Dining, and Telecom.
- **Lifestyle & Travel:**
  - **Lounges:** Unlimited access to 1,200+ lounges.
  - **Salik:** Auto top-up facility.
  - **Ladies Club:** Free access to Sharjah Ladies Club.

### Emirates Islamic Skywards Black Credit Card
- **Type:** Premium Travel Card.
- **Rewards:** Up to **3.5 Skywards Miles** per USD.
- **Elite Status:** Automatic **Silver** membership; Fast Track to **Gold**.
- **Lifestyle & Travel:**
  - **Golf:** Unlimited complimentary golf.
  - **Lounges:** Unlimited access for cardholder + guest.
  - **Valet:** 3 free services/month.

## 3. First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) Credit Cards

### FAB Rewards Active Credit Card
- **Annual Fee:** **AED 300**.
- **Rewards:**
  - 25 Rewards per 1,000 daily steps (via STEPPI).
  - 5 Rewards per AED 1 on sports; 2 per AED 1 on others.
- **Lifestyle:**
  - **Gyms:** Free access to 25+ gyms/classes (ADV+).
  - **Discounts:** 20% off Sun & Sand Sports online; 20% off Talabat (once/month).
- **Travel:** Unlimited access to 25+ airport lounges.

### FAB ADNOC Rewards Credit Card
- **Annual Fee:** **AED 300**.
- **Welcome Bonus:** **AED 300** (as ADNOC Points).
- **Rewards:**
  - **15% back** on ADNOC fuel/services.
  - **3% back** on Salik/Darb/Mawaqif.
  - **1% back** on Groceries/Supermarkets.
- **Lifestyle:**
  - Fast track to ADNOC Gold Membership.
  - Movies from AED 20.
- **Travel:** Global airport lounge access via Mastercard.

### FAB Z Credit Card
- **Annual Fee:** **Free for 1st year**.
- **Memberships:** Free **Careem Plus** & **Noon One** (1 year).
- **Entertainment:** **20% off** Netflix, Spotify, Shahid, etc.
- **Dining:** 20% off Talabat orders.
- **Financial:** 0% foreign transaction fees; low interest rate (1.99%/mo).
- **Travel:** Complimentary airport lounge access.
